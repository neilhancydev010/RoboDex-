My Data & Local Storage Management

You own 100% of your learning data. Export summaries, download versioned backups, restore progress on another browser, or erase a local profile anytime.

⚠ Why Regular Backups Matter

RoboDex́ stores your structured learning records in IndexedDB and simple UI preferences in localStorage. Because there is no remote server, clearing browser site data, using incognito mode, or switching devices will erase local progress unless you save a Download Backup file first.

Delete Local Profile

Permanently remove the currently active student profile and all of its IndexedDB records from this browser. Other local profiles remain untouched.
